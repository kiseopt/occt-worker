# Code of Conduct

[中文说明 / Chinese translation](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

## Expected behavior

Participants are expected to be respectful, precise, and constructive. Assume
good intent, focus criticism on the technical work, and make room for different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, and deliberate
disruption are not acceptable. Do not publish private information or
proprietary CAD data without permission.

## Enforcement

Report a conduct concern privately to the maintainers through the repository's
hosting platform. Public issues are for technical discussion and should not be
used to expose personal information or conduct reports. Maintainers may remove
content or restrict participation when needed to protect the project and its
participants.
