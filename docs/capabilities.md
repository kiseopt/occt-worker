# Public CAD platform capability matrix

[中文说明 / Chinese guide](capabilities.zh-CN.md)

This matrix defines the implemented public surface backed by the OCCT 8.0.1 source tree pinned by this repository. It does not claim to bind every non-visual OCCT class or toolkit. Visualization (`TKV3d`, `AIS`, `TKOpenGl`, `TKMeshVS`), generic OCAF authoring, broader TKXMesh/TKExpress APIs, and pthread/TBB execution are outside the v1 public surface. An item is complete only for the behavior and limits stated below, when it is exposed through the protocol and typed API, exercised against the release wasm, documented, and covered by host integration tests where the host boundary is relevant.

## Kernel and topology

- Complete: scoped generation-checked `TopoDS_Shape` handles, buffers, batch execution, validation, topology counts, indexed subshape catalogs, adjacency, materialized subshape handles, and direct compound/shell/compsolid assembly.
- Complete: primitives including wedges and half-spaces, wire/face/shell/solid construction, core booleans, section, non-destructive splitter workflows, General Fuse cells with source-input mapping and take/avoid/material selection, internal-boundary removal and container construction, extrusion, revolution, loft, pipe sweeps, multi-face draft angles, `BRepFeat` local prism add/cut with length, until/from, semi-infinite, and thru-all limits, local revolution with angle/until/from/thru-all limits, cylindrical holes with all native limit modes, face-selected defeaturing, fillet, chamfer, hollow, offsets, sewing, healing, same-domain unification, transforms, and transform arrays.
- Complete: N-side surface filling from closed boundary wires with optional interior point constraints and G1/G2 support-face constraints, plus arbitrary outer/hole wire trimming on existing surfaces with generated pcurves.
- Complete: planar rib/slot features through native `BRepFeat_MakeLinearForm`, including one-sided or opposing thickness vectors and add/cut semantics.
- Complete: shape-level ShapeUpgrade operations for continuity, angular, area, closed-face, and closed-edge division; selective Bezier conversion; internal-wire removal; and location removal, with the native precision, segmentation, conversion, and edge-selection controls. Complete: native planar and revolution rib/slot features through `linearForm` and `revolutionForm`, plus explicit face/edge-bound `glue`/imprint workflows through `BRepFeat_Gluer`.
- Complete: chamfer exposes scalar and per-edge first/second distances with explicit reference-face control.
- Complete: fillet exposes scalar and per-edge radii, linear start/end evolution, and shared or per-edge multipoint radius laws.
- Complete: extrusion, revolution, loft, pipe sweeps, fillet/chamfer, sewing, shape repair, same-domain unification, and rigid, scale, mirror, and general affine transforms expose full local face/edge history with retained subshapes, OCCT generated/modified replacement mappings, and deleted input subshapes. Pipe-shell sweep history is partial because OCCT does not map an auxiliary spine; its primary spine and profiles remain mapped. The parametric SDK composes booleans, cylindrical holes, defeaturing, extrusion, revolution, loft, pipe and pipe-shell sweeps, fillet/chamfer, sewing, shape repair, same-domain unification, and transforms. Other feature paths use deterministic signature matching and explicitly diagnose missing or ambiguous results.

## Curves and surfaces

- Complete: line, arc, circle, ellipse, hyperbola, parabola, offset, Bezier, BSpline, and cylindrical helix edge construction, with finite topology domains for unbounded conics.
- Complete: edge parameter domains; point, first/second derivative, tangent, and curvature evaluation.
- Complete: analytic curve type/parameter extraction, Bezier/BSpline degree and size metadata, complete curve pole/knot/multiplicity/weight extraction, copy-on-write pole editing, direct BSpline knot insertion/removal, degree elevation and approximation-based degree reduction with reported maximum error, periodicity conversion, batch control-net editing, global curve and U/V surface continuity, finite curve trimming, and exact curve-to-BSpline conversion modes.
- Complete: restricted face UV domains; point and first/second derivative evaluation; oriented normal and principal/mean/Gaussian curvatures.
- Complete: directly bounded analytic plane, cylinder, cone, sphere, and torus faces; finite linear-extrusion, revolution, ruled, and offset surface faces; analytic surface type/parameter extraction; topology-clipped U/V iso-curves.
- Complete: topology-bounded point projection onto curves/surfaces, curve/curve-surface/surface extrema, and topology-clipped curve-curve/curve-surface/surface-surface intersections. Parallel extrema are explicitly identified and return representative global-nearest solutions because the stationary set is continuous.
- Complete: finite rectangular surface trimming with explicit topology limits, topology-preserving conversion to BSpline and copy-on-write pole editing, direct U/V BSpline knot insertion/removal, degree elevation and approximation-based degree reduction with reported maximum error, periodicity conversion, exact affine curve/surface reparameterization with rebuilt boundary pcurves, direction exchange/reversal, batch control-net editing, arbitrary wire/pcurve trimming through `makeFaceOnSurface`, Bezier/BSpline surface construction, point-set BSpline curve/surface approximation with optional OCCT variational length/curvature/torsion fairing, bounded curve/surface extension, N-side filling with C0/G1/G2 constraints, and complete pole/knot/multiplicity/weight extraction.

## Parametric CAD

- Complete: serializable parametric model, safe named arithmetic parameters and dependencies, feature DAG ordering and cycle checks, deterministic serialized recompute, atomic result replacement, rollback on failure, and scoped resource cleanup. Features cover primitives, polygon/face profiles, extrusion/revolution, loft, pipe sweep, fillet, chamfer, hollow, offsets, draft, local prism, cylindrical holes, defeaturing, topology repair, same-domain unification, section, split, sewing, transforms, and boolean cut/fuse/common.
- Complete: serializable expression-driven Bezier and BSpline curve/surface control geometry, including rational weights and explicit BSpline degree, knot, multiplicity, and periodic options.
- Complete: parametric per-feature status diagnostics and suppression semantics; suppressed features are omitted from recompute and dependent features report a failed diagnostic without publishing a partial scope.
- Complete: versioned parametric serialization writes schema v1, migrates the original unversioned representation, and rejects unsupported future versions. Serializable face/edge references persist geometry-family and parent-normalized-bound signatures, re-resolve uniquely after parameter recompute, diagnose missing or ambiguous candidates, and can select topology for downstream fillet, chamfer, hollow, draft, local prism/revolution, and defeature features. References can also name an upstream reference as their `source`; the model automatically captures and composes boolean, cylindrical-hole, defeaturing, extrusion, revolution, loft, pipe and pipe-shell sweep, fillet, chamfer, sewing, shape-repair, same-domain-unification, rigid-transform, scale, mirror, and general-affine OCCT histories across the unique feature-graph path, including multi-input operations, and explicitly reports deleted, unmapped, or one-to-many mappings. Pipe-shell auxiliary-spine topology uses the same explicit unmapped diagnostic because OCCT does not expose its history. Symmetric equal-signature candidates can opt into an explicit `disambiguation: "initialIndex"` tie-breaker when topology ordering is part of the model contract; intentional geometry-family replacement can opt into unique normalized-bound matching. Strict geometry matching and ambiguity remain the defaults. Operations without usable OCCT history use the deterministic signature rule and report ambiguity instead of claiming an impossible cross-operation universal B-Rep name.
- Complete: serializable 2D line/circle/arc/BSpline sketch entities, construction geometry, geometric constraints (coincident, fixed, horizontal, vertical, parallel, perpendicular, tangent for line/circle/arc pairs, concentric, symmetry, equal length/radius), dimensional distance/length/radius constraints, per-constraint residual diagnostics, nonlinear solver diagnostics, arbitrary sketch planes, and sketch-to-wire/face/feature references. BSpline endpoint point constraints are supported; BSpline tangency is outside the bounded v1 sketch solver and unsupported combinations are rejected explicitly.
- Complete: explicit ordered multi-profile sketch output with stable `sketchId.profileId` wire references, compound sketch results, and downstream outer/hole Face and solid-feature use.
- Complete: serializable parametric assemblies, reusable feature component instances, parameter-driven transforms, product hierarchy, names, colors, layers, visibility, physical materials, PBR visual materials, and validation properties mapped to the existing STEP/XCAF document export model.

## Data exchange

- Complete: BREP import/export bound to an exact wasm hash.
- Complete: shape-only STEP import/export with explicit model units and deterministic header timestamp.
- Complete: binary STL export from shapes and validated indexed triangle buffers.
- Complete: STEP XCAF import/export for rooted assembly trees, repeated shape instances, local transforms, label-level names, RGBA colors, layers, visibility, physical material name/description/density metadata, area/volume/centroid validation properties, zero-based face/edge presentation styles, and geometry handles. STEP maps PBR visual materials to presentation color because the format bridge cannot preserve the full metallic-roughness model. Native XCAF binary/XML documents additionally round-trip bounded named-camera `views` records (including clipping-plane labels), explicit component-path `shuo` records with optional per-occurrence RGBA style, datum metadata links, graphical annotation presentation shapes/names for dimensions, datums, and geometric tolerances, and geometric tolerance records with type, value, semantic name, linked datum indices, material requirement, zone modifier/value, all public OCCT modifiers, and maximum-value modifier. XML uses a validated hex metadata marker to restore records and BREP annotation topology omitted by the pinned XML driver. STEP export rejects datum and geometric-tolerance links when the pinned transfer cannot preserve them. Exchange preserves the names and labels represented by each format; feature-tree persistent naming is the separate parametric SDK guarantee above.
- Complete: IGES shape import/export (`TKDEIGES`) through memory streams, with explicit model units and multi-root count.
- Complete: IGES XCAF document import/export (`TKDEIGES`) through memory streams, preserving rooted shape hierarchy, names, RGBA colors, layers, and occurrence transforms through a validated metadata marker; unsupported semantic annotations (`gdt`, `datums`, `geometricTolerances`, `views`, and `shuo`) are rejected explicitly.
- Complete: STL binary/ASCII auto-detected memory-stream import and binary/ASCII export (`TKDESTL`); default import produces triangulated TopoDS faces and intentionally does not recover analytic surfaces, while opt-in mesh import also returns indexed positions, indices, per-facet normals, ASCII solid names, and exact binary 80-byte headers for direct re-export. STL has no UV, material, or hierarchy preservation.
- Complete: OBJ memory-stream import/export for vertex and polygonal face geometry, including negative indices, independent position/UV/normal corner indices, seam expansion, and polygon triangulation. The default import remains shape-only; opt-in document import returns indexed mesh buffers, preserves contiguous object/group/material/smoothing primitive ranges and material-library references, and parses supported material/color/map fields from supplied MTL companion resources. Symmetric document export writes indexed buffers, primitive state, and supported material fields to OBJ plus a companion MTL resource; the shape-only compatibility export remains available.
- Complete: PLY 1.0 memory-stream import and export for ASCII, binary little-endian, and binary big-endian data with vertex positions and polygonal faces; the shape path retains vertex normals and common UV property names in imported face triangulations, while the opt-in indexed mesh-document path preserves u8 RGBA vertex colors plus ordered `comment` and `obj_info` header metadata for direct export.
- Complete: glTF 2.0 JSON and GLB memory-stream import/export with embedded or companion buffers, triangle lists/strips/fans, active-scene hierarchy transforms and instances, NORMAL/TEXCOORD_0 attribute retention, sparse accessor base/override decoding for positions, normals, UVs, indices, and morph targets, POSITION/NORMAL morph evaluation with explicit/node/mesh weights, four-influence linear blend skinning with inverse bind matrices and normalized normals, selected-animation sampling for node translation, rotation, scale, and weights with STEP/LINEAR/CUBICSPLINE interpolation, and OCCT Z-up/glTF Y-up conversion. Opt-in document import preserves the source JSON (including materials, textures, animations, skins, cameras, extensions, and extras), returns resolved buffers/images and primitive-instance ranges, and accepts valid documents without triangles. Symmetric document export preserves that JSON metadata and resolved resources across glTF JSON or GLB packaging. TypeScript preprocessing decodes `KHR_draco_mesh_compression` and `EXT_meshopt_compression` with pinned runtimes before normalized geometry reaches the wasm kernel.
- Complete: VRML 1/2 shape and indexed mesh import/export (`TKDEVRML`) through memory streams; opt-in VRML import returns indexed positions, normals, UVs, opaque u8 RGBA vertex colors, and triangle indices, and mesh export accepts the same indexed attributes within VRML's format limits.
- Complete: native OCCT/XCAF document persistence through memory streams (`TKBinXCAF`, `TKXmlXCAF`), including full PBR visual-material metadata; XML retrieval uses a stream-safe fallback around the OCCT drivers and is covered by binary/XML round-trip tests.
- Complete: in-memory format probing for BREP, STEP, IGES, STL, OBJ, PLY, glTF/GLB, VRML, and native XCAF binary/XML, including encoding and STEP document-metadata detection.
- Complete: multi-root shape-only STEP/IGES export and independent scoped root handles on import, alongside the combined OCCT result.
- Complete: named TypeScript and generated protocol option contracts for every supported exchange format, including shared shape-tessellation controls, format encodings/versions, STEP/IGES units, document/resource options, native XCAF persistence format, and frozen per-operation defaults.
- Complete: shape-only and XCAF STEP/IGES transfer, VRML transfer, edge tessellation, and shape/mesh STL, OBJ, PLY, and glTF transfer report normalized OCCT progress; the same paths in `WorkerClient` cooperatively observe `AbortSignal`. Mesh exchange cancellation is acknowledged at parser, serialization, and buffer-materialization phase boundaries. Every exchange failure provides stable `details.operation` and `details.format` identifiers.

## Mesh processing

- Complete: face-isolated solid tessellation with normals, indices, face groups, and optional UVs; edge polyline tessellation with topology groups.
- Complete: STL, OBJ, PLY, glTF, and GLB mesh import as valid triangulated TopoDS shapes.
- Complete: direct access to an existing face triangulation and copy-on-write replacement of face positions, indices, normals, and UVs.
- Complete: triangulation validation for missing/empty meshes, non-finite attributes, invalid indices, and degenerate triangles; copy-on-write OCCT BRepMesh rebuild with generated normals.
- Complete: typed-array mesh welding with position tolerance and optional normal/UV seam preservation, area-weighted vertex-normal regeneration, and UV tangent generation while retaining face-group index ranges.
- Complete: deterministic grid-cluster mesh simplification by target triangle count or ratio, with rebuilt normals and retained face-group ranges.
- Complete: midpoint triangle subdivision/refinement for up to eight levels, with retained face-group ranges, interpolated UVs, and rebuilt normals.
- Complete: deterministic indexed boundary-loop extraction and bounded planar-hole filling with explicit rejection of non-manifold, branching, inconsistently oriented, non-planar, degenerate, and self-intersecting boundaries.
- Complete: topology-aware selection maps from rendered triangle and polyline-vertex indices back to zero-based OCCT face/edge indices, including reverse ranges and preservation through built-in weld, simplify, and subdivision operations.
- Complete: OBJ and glTF provide symmetric mesh-document import/export with their format metadata and companion resources; PLY preserves positions, normals, UVs, u8 RGBA colors, and ordered global header metadata; VRML preserves indexed positions, normals, UVs, and opaque u8 RGBA colors; and STL preserves facet normals, ASCII solid names, and exact binary headers. These guarantees cover the public mesh/document API within each format's inherent limits. STL and PLY indexed exports reject out-of-range, repeated-index, non-finite, and geometrically degenerate triangles.

## Queries and runtime

- Complete: bounding boxes, mass properties, distance, point classification, validity, shape identity, topology type, resource statistics, workers, timeout recovery, and Node/browser hosts.
- Complete: oriented bounding boxes, topology-aware geometric projections/intersections, stationary extrema with parallel-state reporting, and detailed `BRepCheck` shape diagnostics.
- Complete: indexed face/edge/vertex tolerance inspection and copy-on-write tolerance editing by topology level.
- Complete: all minimum-distance solutions with inner-solid state, stable topology support indices, edge parameters, and face UV parameters.
- Complete: pipe-like shape center-path extraction between face or wire sections.
- Complete: section edge lengths, two-sided ancestor-face mapping, isolated contact vertices, intersection approximation, and optional input pcurves.
- Complete: exact B-Rep hidden-line removal through `TKHLR`, with parallel or perspective camera projection and reusable visible/hidden edge compounds in view coordinates.
- Complete: a separate artifact translates the pinned Emscripten Phase 3 wasm-EH instructions to standardized exception references, and Wasmtime 47 runs scope creation, primitive construction, boolean modeling, bounding-box queries, tessellation, structured errors, and resource cleanup through the frozen ABI. The Node release-wasm suite remains the exhaustive operation test; host tests validate their host boundary and representative kernel paths.
- Complete: tessellation, edge tessellation, VRML, mesh exchange, and shape/XCAF STEP and IGES transfers expose progress and cooperative cancellation through a one-word `SharedArrayBuffer`. `WorkerClient.requestShared()` materializes bulk binary results directly into `SharedArrayBuffer`, shared binary and mesh inputs cross the worker boundary without detachment, and the unavoidable wasm-linear-memory copy is explicit. pthread/TBB builds remain outside the single-thread v1 target; browser parallelism uses multiple workers and arenas.
